# Result D: structural associations at fixed habitat proportion

We related each of the six retained structural metrics to mean equilibrium patch occupancy separately at every stored habitat proportion and for every one of the 256 fixed numerical parameter profiles. The source comprises 104 complete trajectories (eight Only-Random and 16 for each directed scenario), 88 habitat levels and 9,152 snapshots. No landscape, parameter profile or equilibrium was newly simulated. All 104 saved occupancy trajectories were hash-checked and reproduced the consumed response array exactly. Snapshot grid hashes and patch counts matched the structural data at all 9,152 identities. Producer sources and the immutable landscape table were checked against the production manifest.

## Equal-scenario estimand and analysis domain

The author confirmed equal scenario weighting on 15 October 2026. Within an H/profile/metric cell, each of the seven scenarios receives mass 1/7, shared equally among its valid paired landscapes. For a value v, its weighted rank is F(v-) + one half the mass tied at v. We take the weighted Pearson correlation of these two weighted mid-distribution ranks. Both the ranks and their correlation use scenario weights. This is a weighted Spearman association under an equal seven-scenario mixture; ordinary unweighted pooling of 8/16 observations would target a different mixture. Ties are retained with average mass ranks, without jitter. The shared-eight sensitivity converges to ordinary Spearman where all eight landscapes in each scenario are valid; independent SciPy checks had maximum error 8.88e-16.

Only landscapes with at least two patches and finite paired quantities are included. To retain the same seven-scenario target, a coefficient additionally requires at least three valid paired landscapes per scenario. H=0 and1 are excluded; H=.975 is withheld because Only-Random supplies only two valid landscapes. The primary domain is H=.005–.925. At H=.925, Only-Random supplies five of eight landscapes, so interpretation is conditional on the remaining fragmented landscapes. Missing or unplanned trajectories are never coded as extinction. The three-observation rule screens missingness and does not establish precision. Structural metrics at excluded landscapes remain missing even when a stored convention supplies a number.

A response range at or below 1e-10 occupancy units, or a constant metric, makes an association undefined. We retain all 256 parameter profiles, including the nine with narrow overall median response spans. Floor and ceiling regions can give poorly informative or undefined associations; their absence is not evidence that spatial configuration is irrelevant. Tables record the within-cell occupancy span, all defined-H counts, and a separate sensitivity restricting summaries to spans at least .05. Rounding responses to1e-8 is a numerical tie sensitivity only (maximum comparable coefficient change 0.263966); it does not replace exact saved-value ranks. The saved edge-density normalisation differs from a cell-area convention by a global positive factor on this fixed raster; rank correlations are invariant to that factor and no absolute edge-density values are reported here.

## Summaries and species dependence

For each profile and metric, we give the median absolute correlation, median signed correlation and dominant-sign fraction across defined H levels. Every defined sampled level has equal weight; these are not H integrals or prevalence estimates. The dominant-sign fraction is max(number positive, number negative)/number defined; exact zeros remain in the denominator. Positive/negative/zero counts and summary availability accompany the values. Summaries of strength must be read with sign and H curves. The source parameter distribution is the adopted stratified conditional log-area sample, not a rectangular LHS or a population distribution of biological species. Each profile retains design weight1/256. Observed bounds: r=0.003588433–271644.9, dispersal half-distance=1.0291762–481.09925 cell-coordinate units. No physical calibration is implied.

Parameter maps use piecewise-linear Delaunay interpolation in normalized log10(r), log10(half-distance), with the adopted exact stepwise domain and the sampled convex hull both masking support. We never extrapolate. All sampled summaries are supplied in the CSV, and all maps have diagnostic versions showing sample locations. Maximum source-node recovery error was 6.11e-16. Interpolated sign fractions and counts describe displays, not new observations. The numerical reference is domain131, as selected in Result B. The optional empirical illustration is domain163, the most frequent best sampled profile in the existing Result-C comparison (Caryothraustes canadensis, Euphonia xanthogaster, Tolmomyias poliocephalus); this display rule uses no Result-D coefficient. It is an illustrative numerical match, not an inferred demographic parameter or universally preferred bird profile. Only the numerical reference receives bootstrap intervals.

## Pointwise bootstrap and seed influence

The preconfigured bootstrap independently resamples two size-eight cohorts: shared seeds1–8 jointly across all seven scenarios, all H and metric values; directed-only seeds9–16 jointly across the six directed scenarios. No parameter profile is resampled. Each draw recomputes scenario weights, both weighted ranks and their correlation; missing rows stay in their original clusters. At least three valid observations counting bootstrap multiplicities per scenario are required. Baseline9–16 trajectories are never created. Saved index matrices use seed2026101504. Percentile limits use linear .025/.975 quantiles of defined draws, conditional on estimability. The intervals are pointwise, not simultaneous.

The initial/increment batches are1000, maximum20000, endpoint tolerance.01, bootstrap-median tolerance.005, with three consecutive stable comparisons and at least95% defined draws. Each eligible cell freezes at its first stable batch. 491/492 reference intervals converged within 20000 draws; 1 were withheld. Three full interval cells were independently reconstructed using literal seed indices and scalar pairwise rank sums; maximum quantile error 3.33e-16. Nonconverged limits remain missing. Numerical bootstrap convergence does not establish sufficient landscape replication.

Across all profiles, the largest whole-seed omission change was 0.232002; 25491/121700 coefficients exceeded the prespecified .05 influence tolerance under at least one omission. Omission made an originally defined coefficient unavailable in 899 omission/cell combinations, recorded separately. Maximum shared-eight to8/16 change: 0.403159; initial-five to8/16: 0.549013. Complete-cohort sensitivities are available alongside every original coefficient; these are descriptive checks, not additional uncertainty intervals.
